🔍 Stage 3: Sanity Check & Script Feedback

Evaluating narrative flow, sentence structure, metaphor clarity, and word count pacing to create polished voiceover script options.

Strong Narrative Arc

The shift from personal crisis (IT market depression) to internal realization (self-mastery) to collective action (educating the outer circle) creates a clear, compelling story line.

Authentic Vulnerability

Acknowledging the impulse to blame university education and instead choosing personal accountability grounds the message in real human experience.

Metaphor Adjustment

"Positronic Library" is sci-fi heavy. Replacing it with "shared knowledge hub" or "living digital library" improves universal accessibility while keeping tech-focused variants intact.

Option A: High-Impact & Direct (Recommended)

~40 Seconds | 98 Words

"When the IT market hit a downturn, I realized the old playbooks were useless. The rules had changed, and I needed to build entirely new connections."

"As AI accelerates, skill expectations aren't just rising—they're skyrocketing."

"Instead of backing down or blaming my university degree, I looked inward. Active self-mastery was the only way forward."

"Real growth isn't a solo journey—it’s collective. It’s like launching a shared knowledge hub for everyone you care about."

"When you level up, you lift your entire community. Educating your outer circle isn't just an option—it's your duty."

Option B: Sci-Fi / Tech Enthusiast Variant

~45 Seconds | 112 Words

"When the IT market hit a depression, I realized the old rules were gone—and I needed to build entirely new connections."

"As artificial intelligence accelerates, skill expectations aren't just rising—they're skyrocketing."

"Instead of backing down or blaming the university I graduated from, I turned inward. Active self-mastery was the only path forward."

"But true adaptation isn't a solo quest. It’s collective. It’s like bringing a positronic library online for everyone in your network."

"When you level up, you raise the standard across every level of your community. Getting this message out became my duty: educating your outer circle is the only way forward."
