🧠 Logic & Rationale: Why Self-Learning & Outer Circle Matter

Explaining why Erdem believes self-learning matters tremendously amid the rapid rise of AI and how educating your outer circle drives systemic progress.

1. The AI Skill Expectation Paradox

As artificial intelligence tools become widespread, the baseline expectation for human skill skyrockets. Routine tasks are automated, raising the bar for critical thinking, adaptability, and continuous domain mastery.

2. Moving From Passive to Active Self-Mastery

Relying solely on traditional degrees or formal institutions leaves professionals vulnerable during economic downturns. Resilience comes from looking inward, taking active ownership of self-learning, and continually acquiring modern competencies.

3. Growth Is Collective, Not Solo

Personal mastery is only step one. True resilience is achieved when individual knowledge is shared, creating a living "knowledge hub" (or positronic library) that elevates colleagues, friends, and family.

4. Duty to Educate the Outer Circle

The core message concludes with a call to action: "Educating your outer circle is the only way forward." Scaling knowledge outward creates broader economic security and community empowerment.
